<objective>
State the direction the catalog never stated, and prove the review machinery works by running
it on exactly these two statements.

The catalog has four roundtrip rows - PROP-35, PROP-37, PROP-38, LEAN-ENC-1 - and every one
says `decode(encode(x)) = x`. None says anything about the converse: whether a byte string has
at most one parse. That is the malleability-relevant direction, and for the wire format it is
**false** - `DecodeVarint.lean:43-44` admits non-canonical LEB128 and truncates ten-byte blocks
`% 2 ^ 64`, `serialize.rs:267` discards `Ct1Ack`'s Boolean, and `serialize.rs:274-277` accepts
trailing bytes on purpose.

The same pass found the forward direction is mis-stated too. PROP-35's roundtrip is false for
epoch 0 and for `Ct1Ack false`, and its domain is corrected here. PROP-37's is false for two
independent reasons - the `as u32` narrowing of `pts_needed` and the byte-vector length gates in
the unchunked deserializers - and its restatement is **deferred to Phase 7**, because
representability alone was proposed for it and shown insufficient in plan review; Phase 1
annotates it rather than narrowing it to a domain that still would not make it true.

Purpose: REV-01 and ROADMAP criterion 4, and the two new whole-structure targets STRUCT-02a and
STRUCT-02b (the rulings of 2026-09-15 split the single canonicity row in two and retired
STRUCT-04 into STRUCT-03). This plan writes and reviews the **statements** and corrects the two
false domains; Phase 3 and Phase 7 prove them.
Output: two new catalog rows, two corrected rows, two statement records, two review artifacts,
two log entries.
